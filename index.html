About OpenPaw

A Robot Built for Every Pet

OpenPaw is an open-source robotics platform designed from the ground up to interact with, monitor, and enrich the lives of dogs, cats, and other companion animals.

Where Open Source Meets Pet Care

OpenPaw is more than a robot -- it is an open hardware and software platform that puts the power of pet companionship technology into everyone's hands. Built on the ESP32-S3 platform with a rich sensor suite including camera, microphone, speaker, motor drivers, and an LED matrix face, OpenPaw can see, hear, move, and express itself.

Whether you want a robotic playmate for your dog, a remote monitoring station for your cat, or a programmable platform for pet behavior research, OpenPaw gives you the complete toolset to build it.

Every line of code, every schematic, every design file is freely available. Fork it. Modify it. Make it yours. OpenPaw is built on the belief that the best pet technology comes from an open community.

Capabilities

What OpenPaw Can Do

A rich feature set packed into an open, hackable platform.

Two-Way Communication

Built-in microphone and speaker let you talk to your pet and hear them respond. Real-time audio streaming over WiFi means you are always connected, whether you are in the next room or across the world.

Live Camera Feed

OV2640 camera delivers smooth video streaming to your phone or computer. Check in on your pets anytime with a live view, motion detection, and snapshot capture -- all over a secure WiFi connection.

Fully Programmable

Powered by ESP32-S3 with ESP-IDF framework. Customize motor behavior, LED face expressions, audio responses, and sensor logic. The entire firmware is open source and well-documented for developers of all skill levels.

Autonomous Movement

Dual DRV8833 motor drivers with differential steering give OpenPaw precise, smooth mobility. Program patrol routes, follow-behind behavior, or create custom movement patterns for interactive play sessions with your pet.

LED Matrix Face

IS31FL3733-driven LED matrix provides a rich, animated face with expressive eyes and moods. Display icons, animations, or real-time status indicators. The face is fully customizable through software.

Built by a Community

OpenPaw thrives on contributions. From hardware revisions to software features, translations to documentation, the community drives every improvement. Join developers, makers, and pet lovers building the future together.

Getting Started

From Zero to Robot in Four Steps

OpenPaw is designed to be accessible whether you are a seasoned engineer or a curious maker.

1

Gather Components

Source the ESP32-S3 board, OV2640 camera, DRV8833 motors, chassis, and sensor modules. A complete bill of materials is available in the repository.

2

Build the Hardware

Follow the detailed assembly guides with step-by-step instructions, wiring diagrams, and 3D-printable chassis files. No specialized tools required.

3

Flash the Firmware

Clone the repository and flash the firmware using ESP-IDF. Pre-built binaries are available for quick starts, or compile from source for full control.

4

Connect & Customize

Connect to the OpenPaw WiFi access point, open the web interface, and start interacting. Modify the code, add features, and share your improvements.

Hardware

Inside the OpenPaw Architecture: ESP32-S3 Deep Dive

The ESP32-S3 is the brain of OpenPaw, chosen for its dual-core processing, built-in WiFi and BLE, and rich peripheral set. With the OV2640 camera feeding real-time video over the DVP interface, the DRV8833 motor drivers providing smooth differential drive control, and the IS31FL3733 LED driver animating the expressive face, every component was selected for performance, availability, and hackability.
